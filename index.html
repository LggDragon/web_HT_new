Why ad budgets leak silently

Running siloed channels inflates cost per acquisition across every order

When your TikTok creative team doesn't analyze Google Search intent, and your Facebook media buyer lacks server-side data, ad algorithms optimize for the wrong audiences while burn rates remain steady.

  • Browser-side event loss Relying solely on default browser Pixels leads to missing purchase data when users block cookies, causing ad manager attribution to diverge from reality.
  • Creative fatigue in 7 to 14 days A winning early-month creative quickly sees CPA spikes due to high frequency when fresh hooks and iterative variants aren't ready in time.
  • Channels competing for the same audience Without distinct roles for search intent, discovery, and retargeting, Meta, Google PMax, and TikTok end up claiming credit for the exact same conversion.
