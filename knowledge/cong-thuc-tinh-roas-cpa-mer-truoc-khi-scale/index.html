Calculating Break-Even ROAS, Max CPA & Blended MER Before Scaling Ad Spend

You cannot evaluate whether advertising costs are "expensive" or "cheap" without anchoring them against product-level gross margins. This playbook details how to establish reliable financial guardrails before turning on campaigns.

1. Break-Even ROAS (Zero-Loss Return on Ad Spend Threshold)

Formula: Break-even ROAS = 1 / Gross Profit Margin (%)

Example: For an order worth 1,000,000 VND, after subtracting COGS, packaging, and shipping, you retain a 40% gross margin (400,000 VND). In this scenario, Break-even ROAS = 1 / 0.40 = 2.50x. Any campaign operating below 2.50x ROAS is losing money on every incremental order.

2. Max Allowable CPA (Maximum Cost Per Acquisition / Customer)

Formula: Max CPA = Average Order Value (AOV) × Gross Profit Margin (%)

Using the same example, the maximum allowable CPA to break even is 1,000,000 VND × 40% = 400,000 VND. If your business seeks to capture a 15% net operating margin before fixed operating expenses, your target CPA benchmark must be calibrated to 250,000 VND.

3. Why Tracking Blended MER Alongside ROAS Is Crucial

Formula: MER = Total Net Business Revenue / Total Ad Spend Across All Channels

When orchestrating TikTok, Facebook, and Google concurrently, prospects frequently discover a brand on TikTok, research the company name on Google Search, and finally convert on a Meta retargeting ad. If you aggregate reported conversions from all three dashboards, total reported revenue frequently exceeds actual bank deposits. Blended MER gives leadership transparent visibility over total paid media efficiency against actual topline collections.
