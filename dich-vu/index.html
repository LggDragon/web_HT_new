6-Stage Execution Process

From Account Health Audit to Sustainable Scaling

Stage HTSOLUTIONLLC Deliverables Client Deliverable Output
1. Audit & Research Evaluate historical account performance, gross unit economics, landing pages, and direct competitors. Account vulnerability assessment & conversion leak report.
2. Tracking Setup Configure GTM, GA4, Meta CAPI, TikTok Events API, and standardized UTM parameters. Bulletproof tracking infrastructure matching events between ad platforms and CRM/website.
